- Question set: `eval/questions.json`, 50 questions, 197 expected rulings, frozen hash `a770f8c678378a66`.
- recall@10: share of questions with at least one expected ruling among the first 10 distinct rulings returned. The expected rulings of a question are alternative answers, so one is enough.
- MRR@10: mean of 1 / rank of the first expected ruling within the first 10 (0 when none).
- coverage@10: share of a question's expected rulings found in the first 10, out of at most 10, averaged.
- Ruling level: several passages of one ruling count once, at the first position. Tied scores keep the searcher's order, which is what a user sees.
- Modes: keyword is FTS5 BM25 only, vector is cosine similarity only, hybrid fuses both with reciprocal rank fusion. No filters.
- Target: hybrid recall@10 >= 0.700.
